Privacy Policy

Data stored on your device

RepLogger works without an account or remote service. Exercise names, workout dates and times, weights, repetitions, rest durations, and exercise feedback are stored in a SQLite database on your device. RepLogger does not transmit this information to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

Permissions and third-party software

RepLogger does not request access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, tracking, or health records. It uses Expo and React Native software to run the app, store local data, keep the active workout screen awake, provide optional haptic rest-timer cues, and present the operating-system share sheet when you request an export.

Backups and exports

Your operating system may include RepLogger data in an encrypted local or cloud backup according to your device and account settings. RepLogger does not independently control or access those backups.

You can create a readable JSON export from Settings. The destination is chosen through the operating-system share sheet. RepLogger does not upload the export. Protect exported files according to your own privacy needs.

Deleting your data

“Clear Local Data” permanently removes workouts, sets, feedback, in-progress drafts, and custom exercises from the device, then restores the built-in starter exercise catalogue. Deleting RepLogger also removes its local data unless the operating system later restores that data from a backup.

Children’s privacy

RepLogger does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not operate a data-collection service.
