RepLogger

Fast workout logging built for the gym floor.

Record sets quickly, resume interrupted workouts, and review straightforward strength and training-frequency trends—without creating an account.

Focused on lifting

  • Log weight and repetitions in kilograms or pounds.
  • Use an optional rest timer with haptic cues.
  • Resume unfinished workouts and exercises.
  • Review workout history and exercise progress.
  • Export a readable JSON copy of your data.

Private by design

RepLogger stores workout information on your device. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or backend, and only shares an export when you explicitly choose a destination through the system share sheet.
